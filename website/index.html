Engineered for Velocity

Designed for Power Users Who Value Every Millisecond

Traditional clipboards force you into tedious window-switching cycles. C-Slot eliminates the cognitive friction by transforming repetitive copy-paste into rapid-fire muscle memory.

Sub-Millisecond Native Keystrokes via Win32

Under the hood, C-Slot binds directly to Windows user32.dll via high-performance C++ foreign function interface (koffi). It inspects modifier states in real-time with GetAsyncKeyState and simulates instantaneous keybd_event keystrokes with zero lag.

Multi-Monitor Aware

Working with 2, 3, or 4 displays? C-Slot reads your exact cursor coordinates and pops up smoothly in the bottom-right corner of whichever monitor your eyes are currently on.

100% Volatile RAM Isolation

Your copied passwords, API keys, and sensitive documents never touch your hard drive. Slots live exclusively in volatile RAM memory and disappear when you quit or clear them. Zero network calls, zero telemetry, zero logging.

Universal Dual-Key Architecture

Whether you're on a compact ultrabook with only a top number row or at a mechanical desktop workstation with a dedicated Numpad, C-Slot binds to both simultaneously (Ctrl+1–9 and Ctrl+Num 1–Num 9).
